About NexEra Youth Network

NexEra Youth Network is a community for young people who want to learn, meet like-minded peers and grow together. It was founded by Fahad Abdur Rahman, who serves as its founder and chairman.

It is one of the three youth organizations built by Fahad Abdur Rahman, alongside TalentVerse Bangladesh and Visionary Youth Alliance, all guided by the belief in "Better Youth, Better Tomorrow".

Focus

What it stands for

Learn

Creating spaces where young people can pick up new knowledge and skills.

Connect

Bringing young people together to share ideas, support each other and collaborate.

Grow

Helping members turn what they learn into personal and community progress.
